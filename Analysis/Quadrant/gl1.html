There are three projects that I place on GL-1, which are Tagfish from theatre group Berlin, my own works Journal: The Tunnel and My Restaurant. These three works are all related to representation of something happened in the past, by composing the recorded videos, projecting the videos in another environment which is different from the original one or both. It is a way to present the content by recomposing the real, but not current time. My Restaurant, a performative presentation for my semester assessment, involves me as an actor to serve the food on a long table for the two guests who are my program tutors. The work Tagfish, [P.5] has a story that happens in a conference room, but there are only two human actors on stage. All the people involved in the meeting are presented by videos of their individual interviews. The whole conversation is presented by a series of well- composed videos. In Journal:The Tunnel, it is an one-minute video collection from the same site in Utrecht. I captured the things that happened at the site through camera: a sound, a reflection or a water drop. In another space, an old factory in Culemborg, I projected the videos on the glass of a rusted metal door.
