CONCLUSION AND FURTHER 3.

One-direction way
Forking Paths

When I look back to the whole process (thinking, reading, making and writing) during this two-year-research, I see that I have gone through a developmental process. I feel that I am going towards a direction that I have been longing to go for for a long time. I am excited but also face uncertainty sometimes. Even once in a while, I feel that I might be doing something wrong. I think that these doubts and uncertainty come from the fact that theatre and performance are not part of my study background, I felt inconfident to discuss about other artists' projects or theatre groups'. Once I mentioned this feeling during a meeting with Nirav Christophe and Falk Hübner, They told me that doing research is a way that you are telling others how you look at things and people so as to start a discussion. Their feedback was very helpful in the sense that I was worried that I might have the wrong way to look at others' work, yet now I realize there is actually not one right or wrong way to look at performances. So this research is about understanding more how I make my projects and how I look at other works.

This research centers around the quadrant that I have developed as an analytical tool. I started from a simple prototype diagram (Diagram 1.), and throughout the whole process of analyzing and making process, I have slowly adjusted it, forming the quadrant more precise and more close to how I want it to be (Diagram 4.).

When I look at the trajectory of my works on the quadrant, it seems that I am moving from Real Time side towards Fictive Time side in the process, on the Y-axis, I am moving more towards the middle which is, as I mentioned in the introduction, because I want to find the transiting point where spectators change their perception of Human Performer - Non-human Performer or Real Time - Fictive Time or vice versa.

In the last part of my research, I started to include space into the research. I am trying to discuss what the influence of space is when the performance happens in different compositions of space. As I have already said in the chapter about the space, I realized that including space in the analysis make this study immensely more complicated to discuss about. But for sure, it will be studied continually in the further research.

I enjoyed the research process a lot, especially the many new insights that I have gained through the inspiring talks with many people and the critical thinking towards my own thoughts.
