PREFACE

One-direction way
Forking Paths

When I was thinking about the final format for this thesis research, the first thought that came to my mind was questioning the necessity of making a book. Conventionally, books regulate the sequence of the content: either chronologically or according to its developing process. I want to offer the readers the freedom to read my research in the order they prefer. Can it be a box then? Literally, a box, which is filled with different elements, such as pictures, notes, drawings or models? The readers can then take out those elements and examine them based on their preference. After discussing this idea with my program mentor Henny Dorr, she encouraged me to think of more possibilities for the format and also she shared her idea about it: presenting the research in a way that is easily shared and that facilitates conversations or discussions with other people. So perhaps a video, an animation or a website? Through sharing with Henny I was inspired: a document in hyperlinks. What I find interesting about hyperlinks is that the person who makes this has the initial power for deciding which element (a word, an image or a quote etc.) connect to which other element, yet simultaneously, the writer also allows the reader multiple ways to read the document that they can read the research and the parts of the research in different orders. As an artist and the writer of this artistic research, I see many linkages among my works and other materials. For example, I was inspired by performance A and by article B, I aroused a question, so I made project C to try to find the answer; and further, because of project C, and read article D, visited exhibition E, and I wanted to see performance A again. You might feel like losing your way through all the links as you read this document, yet rest assured: it’s also part of the journey. After all, everything is related to everything.

YiLing Hung MARCH 2016
