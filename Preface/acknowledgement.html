ACKNOWLEDGEMENT

One-direction way
Forking Paths

It is a luxury to be back in school as a student and to continue the things that I have set in pause since I graduated from university. Being part of this two-year Scenography program was a great adventure which I enjoyed a lot. For a student like me who is not from a theatre background, I was so inspired by the diversity of performances, workshops, lectures and the experiences. They broadened my views on performance study.

I would like to express my grateful appreciation to many individuals:

Thanks to my two program mentors, Henny Dorr and Tjallien Walma van der Molen, your support, guidance and respect, not only helped me on the development of research, but also, more importantly, gave me lots of strength and confidence.

My program colleagues, Anja Podreka, Dalia Khalife, Hilda Moucharrafieh, Iro Karra, Kataríana Máiková, Maria Vera and the first year colleagues, Astrid Bode, Ana Duarte, Britt van Groningen, Camila Caize, Doro Mines, Fransje Christiaans, Laura de Groot, Nina van der Mark, Samon Presland and Zsofi Hajdu, I have got diverse feedbacks and am inspired by all your individual works during the process.

My external mentor, Masha Ru. Your projects inspired me very much and furthermore your clear feedback helped me to shape the structure of my research document. Your diverse experience make me see the future possibility of continuing making/research with multiple discipline background.

Thesis tutor, Nirav Christophe and project tutors, Vinny Jones and Michiel Voet, all your feedback were a crucial nutrient for the research and most importantly, your positive encouragement supported me to go through some difficulties during the process.

Ministry of Education in Taiwan, I appreciate you granted me a scholarship for studying. Without it, I would have not been so focused on doing research and making projects.

The last, I would like to give special thanks to my dear partner Oscar van den Bosse. You always stand by me and are so enthusiastic on sharing your ideas about the projects. Not only that, you used your knowledge of language and rich experience on editing papers, that helped me greatly on improving the quality of my writing. And my family, parents and sisters, thanks to your support and care I was able to go through the homesickness which I felt more than once.

YiLing Hung May 2016
